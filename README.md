# Remote Work Impact Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard explores the effects of remote work on employees, analyzing key metrics such as mental health, age distribution, social isolation, and access to support resources.

Built using data from a population of 5,000 employees, this dashboard aims to support HR departments, researchers, and policymakers in understanding how remote work shapes the employee experience.

## Key Insights
- Employee Population Size: The dataset contains information on 5,000 employees across various work modes.
- Average Age: The average age of employees is 41 years, suggesting a mature workforce.
- Remote Workers Dominate: Remote workers make up the largest group, accounting for 34.28% of the total workforce analyzed.
- Access to Mental Health Resources: Despite the growing number of remote employees, they do not have substantial access to mental health support, as shown in the data.
- Social Isolation: Remote workers reported the highest levels of social isolation, highlighting a key challenge associated with remote work environments.
- Mental Health Status: Interestingly, despite lower access to mental health resources and high isolation ratings, remote workers' overall mental health appears intact and stable, possibly due to personal coping mechanisms or flexible work benefits.

## 📈 Visualizations Used
The dashboard uses a combination of compelling and easy-to-interpret visual elements:

- 📊 Bar Charts – To compare work types (remote, hybrid, onsite) against metrics like access to mental health and social isolation levels.
- 📉 Area Charts – To show trends across work types and highlight proportions and impacts over time.
- 🧁 Pie Charts – To visualize the distribution of work types, highlighting the dominance of remote workers in the dataset.
